## 📥 How to Make a Bank Deposit

1. Open the app and tap **Deposit** from the bottom bar.
2. Enter your **1XBet Player ID** (e.g. `84920194`).
3. Select your bank from the list:
   * Bank of Ceylon (BOC)
   * Commercial Bank
   * Hatton National Bank (HNB)
   * Sampath Bank
   * People's Bank
   * LOLC Finance
   * iPay / Frimi
4. Enter deposit amount (Minimum: **500 LKR**).
5. Transfer money to the displayed agent bank account.
6. Enter your Bank Slip Reference Number and Sender Name (or upload slip image).
7. Tap **SUBMIT DEPOSIT FOR VERIFICATION**.
8. You will receive a push notification when your deposit is approved and credited!

---

## 📤 How to Request a Withdrawal

1. Tap **Withdraw** from the bottom bar.
2. Enter your **1XBet Player ID**.
3. Enter withdrawal amount (Minimum: **1,000 LKR**).
4. Select your bank and enter your **Account Number** and **Account Holder Name**.
5. Enter your **Secret Code** (1XBet Payout Secret / රහස් අංකය).
6. Tap **SUBMIT WITHDRAWAL REQUEST**.
7. Cash will be transferred to your bank account within 5-15 minutes!

## 📊 Viewing History & Exporting CSV

1. Tap **History** in the bottom bar.
2. Search or filter by "Deposits", "Withdrawals", or status ("Approved", "Pending", "Rejected").
3. Tap **EXPORT CSV** to save your transaction history as a CSV file or share it via Telegram/WhatsApp.
